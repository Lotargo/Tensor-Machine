## Task 4.5.1 Instruction Limit

Implemented a deterministic watchdog (instruction counter) in the IR VM execution loops (`mcp.rs:execute_program` and `vm.rs:VM::run`) to prevent unbounded execution, particularly from infinite recursion in recursive `IfConfidence` branching.

- Passed `&mut instruction_count` through the VM to enforce a strict upper bound of 100,000 instructions.
- Modified tests to use a local `0` instruction count and pass limits.
- Added a `test_instruction_limit` to explicitly verify that the execution terminates and returns an error when the limit is breached.
